Soundslo turns a written scene into an instrumental score with Stable Audio 3 Medium—running on your own Mac or Windows PC.

The desktop app includes its own runtime, prepares the right engine for your hardware, and downloads the pinned model on first launch. Your local prompts, generations, and library stay on your computer.

First launch finishes the setup.

Leave at least 8 GB free and keep Soundslo open while Medium downloads. Progress appears in Settings, failed downloads can be retried, and the model cache survives app updates.

Release notes and checksums

Builds are currently unsigned. On macOS, Control-click Soundslo and choose Open. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway. Later updates install from inside Soundslo after checksum verification.
